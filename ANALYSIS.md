# Website direction used for this build

## OmiChef D2C observations

- Primary categories: Triply Cookware, Non-Stick Triply, Cast Iron, Drinkware, Pressure Cooker and Cookware Sets.
- Strong visual identity: warm cream backgrounds, rust/copper-brown buttons, deep slate-blue typography, stainless-steel product photography and peach gradients.
- Core product trust messages: triply construction, safety certification, durability, compatibility across cooktops, warranty, toxin-free materials and after-sales support.
- Existing Shopify remains the source for collection browsing and product-level shopping.

## B2B adaptation

- Replaces retail discount-led messaging with wholesale value: volume pricing, mixed-SKU cartons, institutional supply, quote support and repeat procurement.
- Uses appointment and lead forms rather than an online wholesale checkout, making it suitable for distributors, retailers, hotels, restaurants, gifting companies and marketplaces.
- Collection cards link directly to the corresponding OmiChef Shopify collections.
- Admin dashboard allows non-developers to update content, links, images and colors.
